07 · What we learned

Four things we now know for certain.

01

Radiology buyers do respond to paid social.

Decision-makers from hospitals, imaging centers and radiology groups opted in and booked.

02

The offer matters more than the ad.

The “12-Week Roadmap” report was the clear winner. Generic benefit claims did not land.

03

The audience is small, so freshness wins.

Results dropped when creative went stale and recovered when we refreshed it.

04

Speed to call is the biggest lever left.

With your long sales cycle, the leads that go furthest are the ones called within minutes.
